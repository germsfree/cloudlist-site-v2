get in touch

cloudlist is made by one person. every email is read and answered, usually within a day or two.

for anything at all, bugs, questions, feature ideas, billing, accessibility, account help, email hello@cloudlist.app. there is no wrong question.

how do i cancel my subscription?

subscriptions are managed through apple, not through us. on your iphone, open settings, tap your name at the top, then subscriptions, then cloudlist, then cancel subscription. your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

how do i restore a purchase?

if you have subscribed before and it is not showing, open cloudlist, then settings, then subscription, then restore purchases.

how do i delete my account?

in cloudlist, open settings, then delete account. this permanently erases all your tasks, streaks, history, and account data from our servers. it cannot be undone.

how do i export my data?

email hello@cloudlist.app and we will help you get it out.

i found a bug

email hello@cloudlist.app with what you were doing when it happened, what you expected instead, and your device and ios version if you have them handy. it helps track things down faster, but send it either way. a vague report is better than no report.

i have an idea for a feature

we would love to hear it. email hello@cloudlist.app. we cannot promise to build everything, but every suggestion gets read, and a good few of the things already in the app started as someone's email.
